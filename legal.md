## Contact form and email

When you use the contact form we receive your name, email address and message, and your phone number if you choose to give it. We use them only to answer you and to follow up on your question. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in answering messages sent to us (GDPR article 6.1 f).

The form is handled by our hosting provider Netlify, Inc., which stores the submission and forwards it to us by email. Netlify processes the data on our behalf under a data processing agreement; it may store data in the United States, covered by the EU Standard Contractual Clauses. We do not sell your data or use it for marketing without asking you first.

We keep messages only as long as we need them to answer you, and delete them no later than 12 months after our last contact, unless the law requires us to keep them longer (for example for an order).

## Launch list

If you sign up for launch updates on the Site, we receive your email address. We use it to send you one email when pre-orders open, and occasional updates on how BrewSpider is coming along. The legal basis is your consent (GDPR article 6.1 a), which you can withdraw at any time.

The sign-up is handled by Netlify, Inc., the same way as the contact form above: Netlify stores your address and passes it to us, under the same data processing agreement and safeguards.

To unsubscribe, reply to any of our emails or write to <a href="mailto:{{ site.email }}">{{ site.email }}</a>; we then delete your address from the list. We keep your address until you unsubscribe.
